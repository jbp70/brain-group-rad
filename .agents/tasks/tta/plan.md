## Detailed requirements per feature (what the reviewer checks)

### FEAT-001
- `linear_flip_signs(flip, task)`: float64 [13] (rigid) / [19] (affine) from the formulas of design 2.1 (S_q = diag(1, f_y f_z, f_x f_z, f_x f_y), entry a_ij sign s_i s_j over the upper-triangle order `[(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(0,3),(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,2),(2,3),(3,3)]`; t sign f; log-scales +1; shears f_x f_y, f_x f_z, f_y f_z). Not a lookup table of Jay's vectors (the test compares to them).
- `encode_linear(R, task)`: float64 numpy QR per matrix with the sign fix of `runtime.metrics.qr_factors`; rotation 10-D via `tfm.encode_10d(Q)` in float64; translation; affine adds `log diag K` and RAW `K01, K02, K12`; rigid drops K and logs max |K - I| at DEBUG; `numpy.linalg.LinAlgError` or det L <= 0 or non-finite -> NaN row (caller marks the flip non-finite).
- `mean_linear(params_list)`: float64 mean in the given flip order; eigh of the averaged symmetric 4x4 (`numpy.linalg.eigh`), smallest-eigenvalue eigenvector q̄, 10-D entries replaced by those of I - q̄ q̄ᵀ; diagnostics per image `rot_gap` = (λ₂ - λ₁)/λ_max, `readout_dev_deg` = angle between `tfm.decode_10d(Q̄)` and the eigh rotation.
- `decode_linear(p̄, task)`: `tfm.rigid_matrix` / `tfm.affine_matrix` in float64, cast float32.
- `linear_tta_level(M_flips, a_prev, centering, task)`: M_flips = list (flip order) of unflipped raw [N,4,4]; per flip A_f = `centering(M_f)` (a_prev None) or `CS.compose_linear(M_f, a_prev, centering)`; R_f = A_f · inv(A_prev) float64; encode, mean, decode; Ā = `tf.cast(centering(R̄), float32)` or `compose_linear(R̄, a_prev, centering)`. Returns (Ā, dict A_f list, residual params, mean diagnostics). Identity-only list: returns exactly today's two-line result (no re-encoding).
- `linear_spread(...)` (design 5 linear rows, D4 names) and `gauge_size(mu_list)` (max_f rotation angle deg / translation mm of mu_id⁻¹ mu_f; mu_f = `tfm.barycenter` float64 with `centering_iters`).
- `train_flip_probs(chain)` -> {level: p or None}: `args.flip_prob` (0 when `args.overfit_one_group`), None when unreadable; WARNING texts of design 7.4.
- `estimate_store_gb(..., tta_levels=())`: unchanged result for the default (test equality with the old value for every branch / level), +28 V × n per TTA deform level inside the existing loop; `affine+deform` keeps its single `src_affine` term.
