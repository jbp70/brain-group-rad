### NIT 1: golden write/compare timestamps in the test log do not match the fixture file

Where: `.agents/tasks/tta/test-log-FEAT-001.md`, Step 1 items 1-2.
Problem. The log says the write ran ~09:39-09:45 and the compare started 09:45:55-05:00. The fixture's mtime is 05:58:55 (Windows and WSL `stat` agree today), and run_tests.sh / the golden test were created at 05:51. A write at 09:45 would have updated the mtime. The ordering requirement still holds (every source edit is at 09:56 or later), but the recorded times are wrong. The likely cause is WSL clock skew at that time, or times written from memory.
Fix. Correct the two timestamps in the log, or add a note that the WSL clock was off and the fixture mtime 05:58:55 is the reliable time of the write.
